# Mathematical Expressions

MarkdownConverter fully supports LaTeX math notation for both inline and display equations.

## Inline Math

The quadratic formula gives us $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ for any equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.

In probability theory, Bayes' theorem states that $P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A) \cdot P(A)}{P(B)}$ where $P(A \mid B)$ is the posterior probability.

## Display Math

### Euler's Identity

The most beautiful equation in mathematics:

$$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$

### Summation and Products

The sum of the first $n$ natural numbers:

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$$

The factorial function expressed as a product:

$$n! = \prod_{k=1}^{n} k$$

### Calculus

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus:

$$\int_{a}^{b} f'(x) \, dx = f(b) - f(a)$$

A common integral:

$$\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} \, dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$$

### Linear Algebra

For a system of linear equations $A\vec{x} = \vec{b}$, if $A$ is invertible, the solution is:

$$\vec{x} = A^{-1}\vec{b}$$

The determinant of a $2 \times 2$ matrix:

$$\det \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} = ad - bc$$

### Number Theory

Euler's totient function satisfies:

$$\sum_{d \mid n} \phi(d) = n$$

For primes $p$, Fermat's Little Theorem gives us $a^{p-1} \equiv 1 \pmod{p}$ when $\gcd(a, p) = 1$.

## Greek Letters and Symbols

Common symbols used in mathematics:

- **Sets**: $\mathbb{R}$, $\mathbb{Z}$, $\mathbb{Q}$, $\mathbb{N}$, $\mathbb{C}$
- **Logic**: $\forall x \in \mathbb{R}$, $\exists \epsilon > 0$, $P \implies Q$, $A \iff B$
- **Relations**: $a \leq b$, $x \neq y$, $A \subset B$, $\alpha \approx \beta$
- **Operators**: $\nabla f$, $\partial f / \partial x$, $A \cup B$, $A \cap B$
